# Billing FAQ

## Why was my card charged twice?
Temporary authorizations can appear as duplicate charges. The pending hold usually disappears within 3-5 business days.

## What happens on payment failure?
We retry failed payments automatically and notify billing admins by email. Accounts remain active during a short grace period.

## How can I download invoices?
Invoices are available under **Billing > Invoices** in PDF format. If download fails, clear cache or try an incognito window.
